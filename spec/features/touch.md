| TOUCH-01 | Android television UI mode chooses TV; other devices choose touch shell. | Android phone/tablet never needs a D-pad to complete a flow; TV retains remote behavior. Native Apple/web chooses appropriate device/input adaptation with equivalent features. |

Observed divergence to resolve when integrating: this phone snapshot hides detail content/actions during pending playback reports and disables Play while loading (`TouchCatalogDetail`, `touchDetailPlayItem`). It does not contain main's later stable pending-action/Retry handoff implementation. Record this as a **gap against CAT-08..13**, not a new parity baseline. Preserve the additive touch behavior while porting main's authoritative guard/queue/freshness semantics. The phone snapshot also predates later main HLS/release/report fixes; those are mandatory shared playback acceptance requirements.
