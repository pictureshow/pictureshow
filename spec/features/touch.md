| ID | Addition/adaptation | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|
| TOUCH-01 | Android television UI mode chooses TV; other devices choose touch shell. | Android phone/tablet never needs a D-pad to complete a flow; TV retains remote behavior. Native Apple/web chooses appropriate device/input adaptation with equivalent features. |
| TOUCH-02 | Home/Libraries/Search/Settings navigation uses bottom bar below 600 dp and navigation rail at/above 600 dp. Player and sign-in hide chrome; IME hides navigation while visible. | Rotate/resize tablet and phone without duplicate destinations or loss of selection. Reselecting the current root returns to that root's top page (the Material 3 and iOS convention); at the top page it changes nothing. The lit destination is the root the page on screen was opened under, stored with the page when it opens rather than worked out afterwards: a root page owns itself, and any other page belongs to the root it was opened from (a library or title opened from Home stays under Home). Phone and tablet tabs keep each root's place when switching (TOUCH-02a). The web and desktop sidebar (and web header) instead open the pressed root's top page every time, from anywhere, and browser Back returns to the page the press left. Bar and rail labels never break inside a word: up to 2x text each stays whole on one line, stepping down only as far as its item's width needs and never below its 1x size. Insets applied once; content is not obscured by keyboard/cutout/nav bar. |
| TOUCH-02a | Tapping the tab you're inside goes to that tab's root, from any screen opened within it. Leaving for another tab keeps the place: coming back returns to the inner screen, and one more tap reaches the root. | Search, a result, Download, Downloads, tap Search: the search screen with its query, not Downloads. Settings, Accounts, tap Settings: the list. Then the same with Home between: the inner screen first. Home keeps its place the same way: a title opened on Home, then Libraries, then Home: the title, and one more tap reaches Home's root. A title opened on Home never appears under Search (`TouchPhoneHomeTest`). |
| TOUCH-03 | Touch pages scroll above IME; width caps at 720 dp; under 480 dp height uses compact title/back/spacing. Minimum common actions 48 dp high. | Landscape with keyboard, large text and small window keeps form submit, Back, dialog choices and errors reachable. Portrait/landscape/split view retain input and screen. |
| TOUCH-04 | Server/manual connection, login, Quick Connect and accounts use Material forms/actions; password Show/Hide; account removal native confirmation dialog. Accounts has one primary action (the current account, else the first remembered, else Add a server); removal is quiet and in the error colour. | Server URL keyboard/no unintended autocorrection; Username Next and Password Done; Show/Hide retains password/selection and accessible label. Back/dismiss cancels removal unless committing. Account identity/expiry semantics match accounts manifest. |
| TOUCH-05 | Dedicated Libraries tab shows every library in a vertical grid of cards, with no sideways scrolling, under one “Libraries” heading; Home shows content shelves with responsive touch cards/grid. | Every library/title is tappable, collection drill-down and paged sorting work, empty states expose Refresh. Responsive rows/cards avoid truncated interactive targets, and cards in a row share one height whatever their data. At 600 dp and wider a shelf's cards fill the row: a row with more cards than fit shows a quarter of the next one, and a shorter row (three Continue watching cards on a tablet) stretches its cards to fill the width, up to 1.5x their phone size; below 600 dp cards keep their fixed width (`TouchTabletLayoutTest`). |
| TOUCH-06 | Library sort is a dropdown (Title, Date added, Release date) with separate ascending/descending action. | Active selection is stated; changing sort closes menu and resets results safely; Back/outside dismisses without changing value. |
| TOUCH-07 | Detail includes responsive hero/metadata/always-present actions with CAT-08..13 pending and Retry behaviour, season choice chips, people/cast/crew cards and person biography/filmography. Touch arranges the actions its own way: Play, with Play from start beside it when there is something to resume or a return is pending; Watched and Favorite; and one Play on TV control whose menu holds Play on TV, Play next on TV, Add to TV queue and Play on TV from start. Play on TV and its from-start form open the Play on chooser (CAST-01) with This device, this viewer's TVs and Cast devices; Play next and Add to queue go to the attached TV, or ask which TV when none is attached. Every action stays reachable. Where the text column is 560 dp or wider, Watched, Favorite and Play on TV share one line, and from 440 dp Download and Find subtitles share the next; a phone stacks them. On a 7-inch tablet in landscape the overview stays in the first screen (`TouchTabletLayoutTest`). On a series page Next up, Seasons and Episodes come before the cast. | Credit person ID routes to Person irrespective of credit kind (Actor/Director/etc.); role/name announced once; biography readable, filmography opens and returns correctly. People metadata absent does not leave empty/dead controls. Once a Play is accepted it shows a check and its requested wording, and every other Play is disabled under its plain name. |
| TOUCH-08 | Search uses labeled touch field, Clear, IME Search, recent chips and clear-history action; grouped results and helpful empty/minimum-input copy. | Query/results/history semantics match CAT-14..16. Keyboard dismissal and tab switching preserve intended query. Retry uses current query, not earlier typed text. |
| TOUCH-09 | Settings is a list: value rows open radio choice dialogs, whole-row switches, and navigation rows for accounts, troubleshooting, support and about. | Includes the Conversion notice switch and “This phone” or “This tablet” (shared report, capture and share). Same defaults/effective values as SET-01..11. Long radio choices scroll (including short landscape with “Scroll for more options”); Cancel/Back/outside dismisses. Selection saves immediately once; checked state is announced. |
| TOUCH-10 | Fullscreen touch player has centered icon-only Back 10 seconds / Play or Pause / Forward 10 seconds over the visible video, a measured scrubber, and one separate native 48 dp secondary icon row: Audio, Subtitles (CC), Speed, Play on when available, the phone orientation toggle, and More. Speed exposes its rate as spoken state. Playback info, Chapters when present, and explicit Stop live in More; Back closes More before hiding controls or leaving, and More holds automatic-next countdown. Cast status and filled mark follow CAST-01; at a title’s end the sender is disabled. Live position uses LIVE-05 and LIVE-PD01. Same playback owner as TV, so PLAY rows hold on touch through one implementation. | Video fills behind cutouts; controls use safe insets. Transport remains above the bottom panel and uses the nearest measured free rectangle within the fitted video to avoid notice and next-episode cards; when no video rectangle fits, use free letterbox space. Both choices clip to measured safe drawing bounds, including side cutouts. Card anchors do not move (vector `player-overlay-layout`). Icon targets stay whole and separate at narrow widths and large fonts. Scrub preview does not seek on every drag; commit seeks once within the real range, and missing duration is safe. A phone opens landscape and offers Portrait/Landscape; windows whose smallest width is at least 600 dp request no orientation and have no toggle. Exit restores the preceding request; crossing that width changes the rule without restarting. Rotation and background preserve the viewing, tracks, subtitle delay, position and pause intent. |
| TOUCH-11 | Touch controls state saves selected sheet/visibility/seek interaction for UI lifetime; idle auto-hide uses the shared 10 s policy (PLAY-04). Back closes an open sheet or the More menu first; otherwise it leaves the player, controls showing or not (phone review P-4; vector `touch-player-back`). Hiding the controls is a tap on the video. The panel's title row starts with a 48 dp Close player arrow that leaves through the same Stop cleanup. | Paused/reader/sheet/scrub states pin controls as implemented; no stray click on underlying video. Stop exits immediately through report/cleanup. Loading/error overlay offers meaningful Back to details. |
| TOUCH-12 | Audio/Subtitles/Chapters/Speed/Playback info are modal bottom sheets with selected radio state where there is a choice, and Done. Chapter rows carry their images when the server has them and open at the current chapter. Subtitle delay Earlier/Reset/Later and burned-in explanation present. Speed and Playback info hold the same content as PLAY-25 and PLAY-26. | Track/chapter pick applies once then dismisses; Done/Back/outside dismisses with no change. Off is a genuine choice (both audio and subtitles). Delay respects bounds and burned-in disablement. All choices remain reachable in short landscape. Below 480 dp height Speed is one segmented row showing every rate; elsewhere it is a radio list opening at the current rate. |
| TOUCH-13 | Skip and next-episode cards are touch buttons; next card Play now/Cancel; screen-reader mode requires manual Play now and the card announces itself. After a return from the background a pending next waits for Play with no countdown. Returning to the foreground paused shows the controls. The card shows a number only while its countdown is running, and that number is real time at the current speed (vector `next-countdown`). Skip and the next card share one anchor at the top end of the player, in every state; while the card is up, Skip sits inside it, after Cancel, so Cancel never moves. On a window too narrow for the conversion notice beside them they sit below it, and stay there when the notice ends until nothing is showing. | Same segment/continuation/completion semantics as PLAY-15..19. No auto-advance while paused/buffering/scrubbing; countdown and physical end each start next once. Captions rise only above the controls panel; the top anchor leaves them alone. |
| TOUCH-14 | Touch About/license/support screens expose same information; license text selectable, active supporter code selectable. | Native text/reading order, store unavailable/fallback and Back behavior remain usable. Platform naming does not claim TV-specific capability on a phone. |
| TOUCH-15 | Picture-in-picture on phones and tablets: leaving for Home (button or swipe up) while a video is on screen shrinks the player into a system window that keeps playing; More also offers Picture in picture. The window shows only the video and captions, with Back 10 seconds, Play or Pause, and Forward 10 seconds. Never on a TV interface, a device without the feature, an error or loading-next card, a finished title, or a viewing handed to Cast or a remote TV (vectors `touch-pip-allowed`, `touch-pip-actions`, `touch-pip-aspect`). | Each action keeps its slot and is disabled when it can't act (live with no time-shift); Play/Pause follows the player's real state, including changes made elsewhere. The window takes the video's display shape, clamped to 1:2.39..2.39:1, 16:9 before the size is known. Expanding returns to the full player with the same viewing; closing the window stops playback through the normal report and leaves the player. Sheets and menus close on entering the window. Platforms without a system picture-in-picture (or where the browser owns it) use theirs and record the difference. |

Touch detail follows CAT-08..13 in touch form: the published page and action grid stay through a playback return, status sits in an overlay with no layout footprint, a tap during the wait is captured with its desired value and runs once in order on fresh data, the 30-second bound is explained, Back abandons, Retry works while loading with a new budget per deliberate Retry, read and write failures stay separate, and a polite live region carries the pending and “Ready.” cues. Touch and TV share one playback owner, so HLS, release and report fixes reach both modes.
