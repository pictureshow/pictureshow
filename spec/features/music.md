# Music library, queue and background audio (launch draft)

**Status: required launch scope. Android reference implemented with vectors, without MUS-10 and the MUS-02 Download action (they follow the offline merge); gapless output (MUS-07), real lock screens and Fire hardware are not yet device-proven. Apple and web are not implemented.** Uses [queue/control](session-control.md), [offline](offline.md), [API contract](launch-api.md) and APP accessibility rules. Evidence: [Jellyfin music organization](https://jellyfin.org/docs/general/server/media/music/) supports tagged music and multi-disc albums; [Plex music overview](https://support.plex.tv/articles/205744257-getting-started-with-plex-music/) documents a music library and multi-disc support. The Plex statement uses its official indexed excerpt; direct article retrieval was blocked. This draft covers general client music intent, not a claim of Plexamp-specific DSP/cloud-service parity.

| ID | Behavior | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|
| MUS-01 | Music libraries offer albums, album artists, artists, songs, genres, playlists, favorites and search. | Page/filter/sort by stable identity without gaps/duplicates or cross-library leakage; preserve browse state on Back. Album artist and contributing artist remain distinct. Empty, inaccessible and failed library are distinguishable. Add music groups to global search without regressing video group order; label media types. |
| MUS-02 | Album/artist/playlist detail shows artwork, metadata and ordered tracks, with Play, Shuffle, Play next, Add to queue, Favorite and Download with OFF-01/OFF-13 platform-specific actions. | Disc then track ordering uses tags, with stable name/ID fallback for missing/duplicate numbers. Various Artists compilations do not split the album incorrectly. Unavailable tracks are readable; playing a selected track starts that entry and retains the selected context. No “playable” container with no tracks. |
| MUS-03 | Mini-player and full Now playing expose title/artist/album, art, elapsed/duration, transport, seek, repeat/shuffle, destination and editable queue. | Same entry IDs/order on each surface. Live/internet audio has no invented duration. Queue changes and Stop are visible/announced without per-second chatter. TV queue has reachable move/remove actions as alternatives to drag; phone has native gestures plus accessible actions. |
| MUS-04 | Explicit audio-only playback continues when browsing, screen locks or app backgrounds. | This intentionally specializes APP-08/PLAY-24 for music only. User Stop, sign-out, queue clear or revocation ends audio. Interruption/audio-focus loss pauses or ducks per OS; resume only if OS permits and viewer did not pause meanwhile. Headphone disconnect pauses; reconnect does not unexpectedly blast speakers. Video does not acquire this exception automatically. |
| MUS-05 | OS media/lock-screen controls reflect current music queue. | Publish title/artist/art/duration/position and available actions; hardware headset Play/Pause/Next/Previous/Seek act once through reducer. Lock screen initial metadata appears with the first track and updates at transition. Stop removes stale control metadata; no other viewer sees cached previous-account art. |
| MUS-06 | Playback negotiates actual audio formats, quality and output route. | Original/lossless is selected only when the negotiated decoder/route supports that source; otherwise offer 128/256 kbps conversion and label output as lossy. Streaming Auto selects from those actual capabilities. No claim of lossless through a lossy Bluetooth/Cast path. Audio-only conversion does not encode video. Recovery retains queue, position and pause intent and reports meaningful source/output facts. A local Music quality preference offers Auto (Android caps a metered network at 256 kbps), Original, 256 and 128 kbps, and applies from the next track opened. |
| MUS-07 | Consecutive eligible tracks play gaplessly on every launch platform, including web. | Eligible baseline is two gapless-tagged compatible tracks at matching sample rate/channels/codec, plus a generated lossless continuous split waveform. Captured output contains no app-inserted silence, overlap or duplicate EOS. Measure encoder-delay handling, resampling/transcodes and remote routes separately; do not use crossfade to pass. Web implements contiguous MSE append or scheduled Web Audio with bounded prebuffering; failure is a launch gap unless a documented spike proves an unavoidable platform limit and the owner revises the row. |
| MUS-08 | Queue obeys QUE-01..08 and restores as paused after process death. | Repeat/shuffle/history/Previous/failure/duplicate rules are deterministic. Selecting Play on a different album with a nonempty queue requires explicit Replace queue confirmation; cancel preserves it. Never overwrite an attached remote queue silently. Audio completion bypasses video episode-next logic. One renderer reports per track; duplicate UI/OS end events cannot double count. A track that plays to its end stops at its full duration; one left by Next, a jump or a pick reports the last position seen (`music-reports`). **Account isolation:** the queue, its saved file and its reports belong to one server and viewer. A step asked for one account never acts on another: a screen read for the previous viewer neither plays, adds to nor asks about the next viewer's queue, a switch ends the old viewing on the old account, and a step for the account just switched to is not dropped for arriving before the switch. Removing a viewer or a server deletes each removed viewer's saved queue, current or not. A 401 on a stream marks the account expired as video does, ends audio and keeps that viewer's queue paused for their sign-in; a new token for the same viewer reopens anything prepared with the old one. Restoring a saved queue never holds Play or Stop behind its server check, stops checking at the first failed request, and doesn't replace a queue the viewer started meanwhile. Controllers that pick a row or set play/pause go through the queue like the screens. |
| MUS-09 | Playlists can be created, renamed, played, appended, reordered and edited when server permits. | Persistent server entry IDs distinguish duplicate songs. Playback queue changes do not mutate a saved playlist without explicit action. Read back successful edits and expose denied/failed/unknown results; do not retry append after ambiguous success without reconciliation. A request that names ids in its URL sends at most 100 per request, in order, so it stays well under the server's 8 KB request line; a failure after a batch was applied is unknown, reconciled by read-back (`music-playlists.long-id-lists-go-in-batches`). Restriction/ownership prevents edits rather than hiding failures. A new playlist is private to its creator: send `IsPublic: false` explicitly, because the server's create default is public and would show it to every user on the server; public only when the viewer explicitly chooses it. A rename doesn't change who can see a playlist. |
| MUS-10 | Native in-app downloaded music uses the same manifest, budget and progress outbox as video. | Album/playlist snapshot order remains available offline; cached tracks need no server token check to start while disconnected. Online-only tracks in a mixed queue explain missing availability; one bounded skip per traversal, then stop. Reconnect does not replace local audio mid-track. |
| MUS-11 | Music works on TV as well as touch/web. | TV album/queue lists, now-playing artwork, reader order and screensaver behavior remain usable by remote at 1.3 text scale. Background audio does not keep video controls/decoder allocated. Music-to-video transition stops audio once before starting video, preserving saved queue for deliberate return. |

## Platform-dependent rows

| ID | Mechanism/limit | Acceptance and fallback |
|---|---|---|
| MUS-PD01 | Android/Fire: ExoPlayer playlist in Media3 MediaSessionService, foreground mediaPlayback service/notification, audio focus and becoming-noisy handling. [Android background audio](https://developer.android.com/media/media3/session/background-playback). | Foreground-service declarations/start policy and notification behavior are tested on supported OS versions and Fire hardware. Activity disappearance does not release intentional music. System kill can end playback; restore paused with clear state. |
| MUS-PD02 | iOS/iPadOS/tvOS: AVQueuePlayer or measured native gapless engine, playback AVAudioSession, background audio mode, MPNowPlayingInfoCenter and MPRemoteCommandCenter. [Apple remote commands](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/mediaplayer/mpremotecommandcenter). | Observe interruption/route changes and release observers once. Real lock-screen/Control Center/headset/AirPlay tests required on iPhone; tvOS Now Playing/Siri Remote replaces a TV lock screen. Native engine choice passes MUS-07 waveform proof. No MusicKit subscription or Apple Music account. |
| MUS-PD03 | Web: persistent audio engine, contiguous MSE SourceBuffer append or scheduled Web Audio for MUS-07, and feature-detected navigator.mediaSession. [Media Session](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Media_Session_API). | Implement gapless eligible tracks at launch; engine prebuffering/cancellation is bounded and track reports occur once. OS lock controls exist only for actions exposed by the browser; list missing actions in release matrix. Autoplay rejection shows Tap Play. Tab suspension/closure can end audio; service worker cannot play it. Browser-original downloads follow OFF-13 and remain external files, without an in-app offline music queue at launch. These lifecycle limits do not waive web gapless. |

## Planned vectors and proof

`music-catalog`: multi-disc/compilation/missing tags, artist versus album artist, paged search and permissions; `music-session`: interruption/noisy/duck/resume intent, lock command deduplication, music→video transition; `music-reports`: one viewing per track, restored paused queue, repeated same track entries, offline replay; queue suites from session-control. Playback continuity needs device audio capture and reference waveform; unit tests prove ordering, not audible gaplessness. Offline, Cast, remote and group music each reuse the same queue acceptance.
